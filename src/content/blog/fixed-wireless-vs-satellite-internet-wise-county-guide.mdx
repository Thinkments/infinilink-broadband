## Understanding the Physics: Earth vs. Space

The fundamental difference between fixed wireless and satellite internet comes down to the distance data must travel.

### How Satellite Works
Traditional geostationary satellites orbit approximately 22,236 miles above Earth's equator. Even lower-orbit constellations orbit hundreds of miles in space. When you click a link, your signal must travel into space and back, creating unavoidable lag (ping times often exceeding 60ms to 650ms) that makes video calls freeze.

### How Fixed Wireless Operates
Fixed wireless utilizes land-based communication towers positioned throughout Wise County. A compact, unobtrusive antenna mounted on your roof maintains a direct line-of-sight link with our local tower. The signal travels just 3 to 10 miles through the air, yielding responsive ping times typically between **20ms and 35ms**.
